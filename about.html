About Peekaboo Planet

Our story

A warm classroom community built around children

Peekaboo Planet Daycare & Aftercare Centre is a nurturing, family-focused early childhood learning centre in the heart of Meyerspark, Pretoria East. Our learners wear a school uniform, promoting belonging, identity, discipline and pride in being part of our Peekaboo Planet family.

We believe every child deserves a happy, safe and stimulating environment where they can discover their potential, build confidence and establish a strong foundation for lifelong learning.

As an English-medium learning centre, we offer quality early childhood education delivered by qualified, caring teachers who understand that every child develops at their own pace.

Our approach

Structured learning, purposeful play and meaningful support

Learning through play

We combine structured learning, purposeful play, creativity, movement and exploration to support children’s intellectual, emotional, social and physical development.

Personalised growth

Upon enrolment, every learner undergoes a developmental and learning assessment so teachers can identify strengths and support individual needs.

Healthy routines

We place importance on nutrition, healthy eating, water intake and daily rhythms that help children feel secure, energised and ready to learn.

Family partnership

Regular updates, progress reporting and photo sharing keep parents informed and connected to each child’s learning journey.

Mission & values

What guides us every day

Love

We create a caring environment where children feel safe, seen and emotionally secure.

Curiosity

We encourage questions, exploration and hands-on discovery that bring learning to life.

Confidence

We help children build real-world skills, independence and school readiness through steady support.

“Our mission is to provide affordable, high-quality childcare, early childhood education and aftercare in a safe, nurturing and stimulating environment. Through personalised learning, purposeful play, healthy nutrition and enriching experiences, we empower children to develop the confidence, creativity, knowledge and life skills needed for lifelong success.”
