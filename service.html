Our services

Support for every family rhythm

Care, learning and enrichment in one place

Daycare

Safe, warm care from early morning to late afternoon with age-appropriate routines and genuine attention for every child.

Aftercare

Structured academic assistance, homework support, reading development and meaningful afternoons for Grade R to Grade 7 learners.

Saturday learning support

Focused support in Mathematics, English, Afrikaans and coding/robotics to strengthen confidence and close learning gaps.

Daily experience

What you can expect from a day at Peekaboo Planet

  • Nutritious breakfast, snack and wholesome home-cooked lunch
  • Movement, sensory play, creative exploration and purposeful learning time
  • Reading support, speech development and confidence-building routines
  • Regular updates and progress reports so parents stay informed
Built around every child

Practical support for growing learners

School readiness

We help children build early literacy, numeracy, confidence and responsibility in ways that prepare them for the next stage of learning.

Academic confidence

Children receive additional attention in reading, spelling, handwriting, vocabulary and foundational skills that support classroom success.

Healthy nutrition

Freshly prepared meals and snacks focus on natural ingredients and healthy eating habits to support growth, energy and concentration.

Parent partnership

We encourage open communication, feedback and regular updates so families remain actively involved in their child’s development.
