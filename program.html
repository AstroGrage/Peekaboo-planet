Programs & age groups

Learning groups

Five carefully structured programmes for growing learners

Twinkle Tots

0–23 months

Warm, secure infant care focused on sensory exploration, communication, movement and emotional wellbeing.

  • Sensory play and early communication
  • Motor skill development
  • Consistent nurturing routines
Rising Stars

2–3 years

Gentle toddler development that supports language, independence and self-help skills.

  • Potty training support
  • Early literacy and concepts
  • Social interaction and motor skills
Planet Explorers

3–4 years

Curious, playful learning with early literacy, numeracy and problem-solving activities.

  • Storytelling and phonics awareness
  • Matching, sorting and patterns
  • Creative thinking and teamwork
Little Astronauts

4–5 years

More structured learning that strengthens focus, independence and readiness for Grade R.

  • Letter and number recognition
  • Pre-writing and listening skills
  • Confidence in class participation
Galaxy Graduates

5–6 years

Grade R preparation focused on phonics, numeracy, comprehension and school confidence.

  • Reading and writing readiness
  • Problem-solving and communication
  • Responsibility and classroom habits
Aftercare

Grade R–7

Academic help, reading development, creative play and supervised learning after school.

  • Homework support
  • English and Afrikaans development
  • Structured, supportive afternoons
Additional support

Saturday school and seasonal learning opportunities

School program only

Saturday learning support

Saturday sessions offer focused help in Mathematics, English and Afrikaans, alongside coding and robotics, to strengthen skills and close learning gaps.

Winter academic bootcamp

During the winter school holidays, our learners join a four-day academic camp designed to build confidence, revise key skills and prepare for the next school term.
