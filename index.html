Welcome

More than care — we nurture, educate and prepare children for the future.

At Peekaboo Planet, we believe childhood should be filled with discovery, laughter and meaningful learning. We bring together excellent care, purposeful play and practical support so families can feel confident and excited about every day.

  • Warm, English-medium learning for little learners
  • Structured routines with play-based discovery
  • Supportive aftercare and Saturday learning help
  • Healthy nutrition, family communication and care
